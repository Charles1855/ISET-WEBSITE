Service Pillars

How ISET puts the sports-education nexus to work

Seven interlocking services carry ISET's mission from concept to classroom to pitch — strengthening institutional capacity, building coalitions, and mobilizing critical resources.

Service Catalog

Comprehensive service streams for sustainable impact

Each service is structured to support teachers, schools, communities, and government partners in unlocking youth potential.

01 · Capacity

Institutional Capacity Support

Strengthening the capacities and systems of educational and sports institutions across Malawi through structured training courses, seminars, continuous assessment, and practical feedback mechanisms.

02 · Coalition

Partnership and Networking

Fostering partnerships with civil society organisations, philanthropic foundations, private-sector bodies, professional associations, sports academies, schools, government ministries, and international bodies — creating a true “coalition of the willing”.

03 · Expertise

Technical Insight and Expertise

Providing technical leadership in concept development, strategic planning, rigorous implementation, and evidence-based review of integrated programmes that bridge school curricula with physical education.

04 · Strategy

Strategic Action for Results & Impact

Developing phased national programmes upon which short-term, mid-term, and long-term strategies and operational action plans are established, geared towards measurable improvements in learner retention and health.

05 · Resourcing

Resource Mobilization for Sports Harmony

Fund-raising and financial stewardship through secondary-recipient funding mechanisms, enabling direct execution of integrated school-sport tournaments, training camps, and facility improvements.

06 · Knowledge

Catalysing Knowledge & Accountability

Supporting knowledge exchange, data collection, and mutual-accountability platforms that cultivate a vibrant, evidence-driven community of practice across the sports-education sector.

07 · Policy

Action for Malawi Education & Sports Policy

Through delegation and advisory roles, actively supporting the implementation of the Government of Malawi's national policies for child protection, adolescent health, and youth development.

Delivery Framework

Integrated Execution at Scale

ISET delivers these services through collaborative clusters — grouping primary and secondary schools in targeted zones to share resources, physical education trainers, and competition pitches. This approach ensures maximum reach, cost efficiency, and long-term community ownership.
