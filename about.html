About ISET

Who we are, and why we exist

Integrative Sports-Education Trust (ISET) is a charitable, public-good organisation dedicated to translating Malawi's vision for its children by enhancing the vital nexus between sports and education.

Guiding Maxim

Mens sana in corpore sano — a healthy mind in a healthy body.

Our Identity

A blend of sport and scholarship

ISET's founders are a blend of long-serving sports professionals, sports lovers, and educationists who believe in taking the maxim “healthy minds in healthy bodies” (mens sana in corpore sano) to a tangible, systemic level in Malawi.

We believe that when physical education and structured athletics are woven into school life, children discover discipline, purpose, teamwork, and an enduring appetite for learning.

Our Motivation

Protecting learners from harm

We are motivated by the urgent moral urge to protect Malawian school-age children from mind-incapacitating vices and dangerous habits — including smoking, alcohol and drug abuse, video gaming and gambling addiction — which frequently force them out of school and render them vulnerable to exploitation, child trafficking, and hazardous labour.

ISET arose out of the conviction that unlocking the potential of field sports such as football, netball, volleyball, tennis, rugby, and squash — and making them attractive, engaging, and entertaining — offers the most reliable route to preserving a child's educational progression and mental wellbeing.

Our Vision

A better-educated, healthier, more prosperous Malawi

Useful, successful, innovative, self-motivated, and enterprising Malawian children and youth who are poised to become the seeds of a better educated, healthier, and prosperous Malawi nation.

Our Mission

Sport as a strategy for youth protection

To leverage the power and vantage of sports as a strategy for protecting Malawian children from indulging in dangerous habits, exploitation, and all vices that hamper their educational progression, pursuit of beneficial careers, health, and wellbeing.

Strategic Objectives

Four goals guiding every programme we build

Every initiative undertaken by ISET is measured against these four foundational pillars.

1. Enhancing the Sports-Education Nexus

Improving Malawian school-going children and youth's educational attainment, lifestyles, and wellbeing by leveraging the vantage of sports within educational establishments across the country.

2. Insulating Learners Against Exploitation

Protecting learners from indulging in dangerous habits, exploitation, and all vices that hamper their educational progression, pursuit of beneficial careers, health, and social wellbeing.

2,000

3. Enhancing Physical Education Teachers' Capacity

Equipping up to 2,000 sports and physical education teachers from across Malawi with modern pedagogy, safeguarding, and coaching certificates in ISET's first three years.

1M+

4. Increasing Sports Activities in Educational Institutions

Benefiting up to one million learners within a ten-year period through institutionalized sports-in-education competitions, clubs, and clinics.
