Partnership Pathways

How organisations & individuals collaborate

We work with diverse international and local partners across multiple engagement models:

Corporate & Institutional Partnership

Join our coalition of civil society organisations, primary and secondary schools, sports academies, and private-sector bodies aligned with CSR and youth empowerment goals.

Funding, Grants & Secondary Recipient Mechanisms

Support structured funding facilities that finance physical education teacher clinics, school tournaments, safe sports kits, and monitoring platforms with transparent financial accounting.

Volunteering, Coaching & Individual Giving

Contribute your time as a sports coach, mentor, curriculum consultant, or make a direct donation toward sports equipment and learner nutrition programmes.

Fiduciary Assurance: ISET is a fully registered charitable trust under the laws of Malawi with independent financial governance and auditing.
