Leadership & Governance

Founded on the pitch, built for the classroom

ISET was conceived by long-serving sports professionals, international civil servants, and educationists who united to fill a critical gap in Malawian youth development.

Founders' Story

An informal meeting with a lasting mission

Integrative Sports-Education Trust (ISET) was conceived in an informal meeting that brought together its founding trio of Dr. Laila Lokosang, Mr. Francis Songo, and Mr. James Chilima Mpunga.

James and Francis played football until they retired and transitioned to coaching careers, training young lads aged eight to fourteen in Lilongwe. Seeing the need to put the skills of these long-serving sports professionals to a greater public good — developing sport to occupy children outside schooling hours — the trio agreed to establish a charity for this purpose, at a time when no non-governmental or civil society organisation in Malawi, or much of Africa, played this role.

Board of Trustees

Meet our Board of Trustees

Distinguished leaders bringing expertise across diplomacy, education, international development, finance, and elite athletics.

LL
Dr. Laila Lokosang
Non-Executive Director / Trustee, Chair

A national of South Sudan and a permanent resident of Malawi for 34 years, Dr. Lokosang is a retired international civil servant who served under three UN agencies in Malawi (UNFPA, WHO, and UNDP) as a statistician and health information systems expert, before serving at the African Union in Ethiopia from 2012 to 2024. An avid football fan, he was Secretary of the Nile Sports Club from 1988 to 1992, and is Founder and first Chairman of ISET's Board of Trustees.

FS
Francis Songo
Non-Executive Director / Trustee, Secretary

Mr. Songo (nicknamed “Falcao”) is a veteran Malawian sports personality whose career began in the late 1980s and grew into a professional role with the Malawi National Team. He played for Malawi Railways FC and later Silver Strikers FC, representing Malawi at U-15, U-18, and Senior National Team levels. After retiring, he became a certified football coach, earning managerial roles at Silver Strikers and the National Team. Francis is a Trustee and Founder of ISET.

JC
James Chilimampunga
Non-Executive Director / Trustee, Member

Mr. Chilimampunga is a former Malawian international football defender for Limbe Leaf FC (Blantyre) and later Silver Strikers FC (Lilongwe) in the 1970s, also representing the Malawi National Team. After retiring from club football, he trained as a coach specialising in youth football, and has also served as a health and wellbeing counsellor. James is a Trustee and Founder of ISET.

BK
Bernadetta Kwacha Kulemeka
Trustee

Mrs. Kwacha Kulemeka is a Malawian educationist with an extensive teaching career, and currently Founder and Director of Cherub Private School in Lilongwe. She has served on national and organisational boards, including as an appointed member of the Teachers Council of Malawi, and in leadership roles within faith-based and community organisations such as Radio Maria Malawi. She is also an associate member of the Medical Missionaries of Mary (MMM).

EM
Ambassador Dr. Ernest Mungo Makawa
Trustee

Ambassador Dr. Makawa served as a senior civil servant in various Government of Malawi positions, including with the Malawi Diplomatic Mission, at one point serving as Malawi's Ambassador to China. He also served as Controller of Legal Services at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Malawi, and is an accomplished, trained legal practitioner.

FG
Future C. Gwaza
Trustee

Mr. Gwaza is a Malawian corporate executive and entrepreneur based in the Johannesburg Metropolitan Area, South Africa, currently serving as Chief Executive Officer at Living Medicals Distribution (Pty) Ltd, a division of Living Group. He has a rich background in corporate banking, with earlier roles at Rand Merchant Bank and Living Group. Future is a passionate advocate of youth sports and community wellbeing.

Governance & Accountability

Rigorous Stewardship

ISET operates under strict fiduciary, ethical, and safeguarding guidelines in accordance with the laws governing charitable trusts in Malawi. All funds, secondary-recipient grant allocations, and programme expenditures undergo regular independent audit and reporting to our partners and donors.
