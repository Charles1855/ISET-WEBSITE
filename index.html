About ISET

Who we are, and why we exist

Integrative Sports-Education Trust (ISET) is a charitable, public-good organisation working to translate Malawi's vision for its children by enhancing the nexus between sports and education.

Who we are

A blend of sport and scholarship

ISET's founders are a blend of long-serving sports professionals, sports lovers, and educationists who believe in taking the maxim “healthy minds in healthy bodies” (mens sana in corpore sano) to another level.

Read Full Story →
Our motivation

We are motivated by the moral urge to protect Malawian school-age children from mind-incapacitating vices and dangerous habits — smoking, alcohol and drug abuse, video gaming, and gambling addiction — which often force them to drop out of school and render them vulnerable to exploitation.

ISET unlocks the potential of field sports such as football, netball, volleyball, tennis, rugby, and squash, offering the best route to a child's appreciation of learning, social interaction, and life.

Our vision

A better-educated, healthier, more prosperous Malawi

Useful, successful, innovative, self-motivated, and enterprising Malawian children and youth who are poised to become the seeds of a better educated, healthier, and prosperous Malawi nation.

Our mission

Sport as a strategy for protection

To leverage the power and vantage of sports as a strategy for protecting Malawian children from indulging in dangerous habits, exploitation, and all vices that hamper their educational progression, health, and wellbeing.

Strategic Goals

Four goals guide everything ISET builds

Strategic benchmarks that connect the sports ground with the classroom.

Enhancing the Sports-Education Nexus

Improving Malawian school-going children and youth's educational attainment, lifestyles, and wellbeing by leveraging the vantage of sports within educational establishments.

Insulating Learners Against Exploitation

Protecting learners from indulging in dangerous habits, exploitation, and all vices that hamper their educational progression, pursuit of beneficial careers, and health.

2,000

Enhancing Physical Education Teachers' Capacity

Equipping up to 2,000 sports and physical education teachers from across Malawi in ISET's first three years.

1M+

Increasing Sports Activities in Schools

Benefiting up to one million learners within a ten-year period through structured school-sport activities.

Our raison d’être

In-School Children and Youth

Our prime, or virtual “end-users” of services and tailored projects are school-going children and young adults aged between seven and sixteen years. Beyond on-field training and coaching, they benefit from group talks, mentorship seminars, and life skills.
