Our Stakeholders

Five groups, one shared purpose

From the child learning teamwork on the pitch to the policy maker who creates the legal framework — every ISET programme is built around the stakeholders who shape a learner's destiny.

Ecosystem Strategy

A holistic, community-wide approach

Sustainable transformation in school sport requires engaging the full circle of influence surrounding the Malawian child.

Ages 7–16
01 · Our Raison D’Être

In-School Children and Youth

Our prime, or virtual “end-users” of services and tailored projects are school-going children and young adults aged between seven and sixteen years. They deserve our full attention from the time they can understand the importance of sports, physical education, and social networking, to the time they become full adults with shaped career ambitions, ready to fit positively into society.

Beyond on-field training and coaching, they benefit from group talks and mentorship seminars run by ISET-trained coaches, mentors, and consultants — learning the value of teamwork, respect, playing within the rules, emotional resilience, and the lifelong rewards of sports-education activities.

Formative & Refresher Courses
02 · Our Multipliers

Physical Education Trainers, Coaches, and Mentors

Alongside the children, their trainers, coaches, and mentors — whether placed in individual schools or working across clusters of schools — are targeted by projects customised around ISET's own training materials and curricula. ISET organises custom courses for Children and Youth Trainers, Coaches, and Mentors as funding allows.

Formative and refresher certificated courses run from a few days to three weeks, incorporating practical drills, safeguarding protocols, and pedagogical assessment. When needed, ISET invites expert trainers from abroad to upskill local facilitators.

Schools & Leadership
03 · Our Institutional Allies

Leaders in Educational Institutions

By leaders in educational institutions, we mean school head teachers, principals, private school board members, school inspectors, curriculum development directors, and heads of educational civil society bodies. ISET pitches the organisation, its motivation, and operational realism to these leaders individually or in groups.

We work with school leadership to ensure dedicated timetable space for physical education, safe sporting infrastructure, and an institutional culture that values athletic achievement alongside academic excellence.

First Point of Contact
04 · Our First Partners

Parents and Guardians

By their very nature, parents are the first line of contact with their children, which makes targeting them critical. ISET prepares community materials to pitch the organisation, inspire confidence, and secure the active willingness and collaboration of parents.

Parents are also vital informants in participatory evaluation exercises, helping us assess behavioral changes, improved attendance, and home support for the child's sporting and schooling balance.

Advocacy & Policy Briefs
05 · Our Enabling Environment

Policy Makers and Government Officials

Policy makers are critical, as together they create the enabling regulatory environment for any major programme's function and delivery of results. A policy framework sets clear goals, promotes inter-ministerial coherence, and ensures programmes operate according to international child protection principles and the law.

Different policy makers are the targets and recipients of ISET's advocacy, policy briefs, and annual progress reports, and are habitually invited to key ISET tournaments and consultative symposia.
